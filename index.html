About

Engineering-focused software development

My work centers on solving practical manufacturing and test-engineering problems with maintainable software.

I build applications that simplify complex procedures, reduce operator error, connect to instruments and embedded devices, validate and analyze manufacturing data, and preserve useful test records.

This portfolio contains independently written demonstration projects using fictional data and simulated equipment.

The repositories are designed to show software architecture, testing discipline, interface design, data analysis, and engineering problem-solving without exposing employer or customer information. Generative AI is used as an accelerator, with final decisions, verification, and implementation remaining under my review.
